# Common Mistakes Checklist

<Checklist>
<Check id="c1">**Negative charges** go **opposite** to $\vec E$ and feel the **opposite** $\vec v\times\vec B$ force. Check for the minus sign every single time.</Check>
<Check id="c2">$V = 0$ at a point does **not** mean $\vec E = 0$ there (and vice versa).</Check>
<Check id="c3">A conductor in equilibrium has **constant** $V$, not necessarily zero $V$.</Check>
<Check id="c4">Capacitance depends on **geometry and material only**, never on $Q$ or $V$.</Check>
<Check id="c5">Capacitors combine **opposite** to resistors.</Check>
<Check id="c6">**Series capacitors share $Q$. Parallel capacitors share $\Delta V$.**</Check>
<Check id="c7">Changing a capacitor? Ask: **battery connected (V fixed) or isolated (Q fixed)?**</Check>
<Check id="c8">"Energy is conserved" is **not** a reason for the capacitor's energy to stay the same.</Check>
<Check id="c9">Energy goes like $Q^2$ or $V^2$: **half the energy ≠ half the charge.**</Check>
<Check id="c10">$\tau$ uses the **equivalent** $R$ and the **equivalent** $C$.</Check>
<Check id="c11">Uncharged capacitor at $t = 0$ → **wire**. Charged capacitor at $t \to \infty$ → **gap**. (Inductor: the reverse.)</Check>
<Check id="c12">A negative current from Kirchhoff just means **the arrow is backwards**. Keep the size.</Check>
<Check id="c13">A battery with current forced **into its + terminal** is **absorbing** power.</Check>
<Check id="c14">Reshaping a wire keeps its **volume** constant.</Check>
<Check id="c15">Arc angles in **radians**.</Check>
<Check id="c16">Wire segments pointing straight at $P$ contribute **zero** $\vec B$.</Check>
<Check id="c17">$\vec B$ from different wires: add as **vectors**, not numbers.</Check>
<Check id="c18">Ampère: only **enclosed** current goes on the right, with **signs** from the right-hand rule.</Check>
<Check id="c19">$\oint\vec B\cdot d\vec\ell = 0$ does **not** mean $B = 0$.</Check>
<Check id="c20">Same-direction currents **attract**; opposite currents **repel** (opposite to charges!).</Check>
<Check id="c21">Magnetic forces do **zero work**: they change direction, not speed.</Check>
<Check id="c22">Net force on a closed loop is zero only in a **uniform** field.</Check>
<Check id="c23">Hall effect: both carrier signs are pushed to the **same edge**. The sign of $\Delta V$ tells you which kind it was.</Check>
<Check id="c24">Gauss/Ampère write-ups: **name your surface/loop** and **what's enclosed**.</Check>
<Check id="c25">Always do a **units** check and one **limiting-case** check.</Check>
</Checklist>
