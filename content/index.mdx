# PHYS 10320 · Exam 2 study guide

Electric potential, capacitors, DC and RC circuits, magnetic fields, and magnetic forces. Each chapter starts from the core idea, builds up the formulas, and ends with worked examples and short quizzes.

>
> **How to use this guide:** if you can explain each *core idea* section in your own words, and work every *worked example* without looking at the solution, you are well prepared for Exam 2. Expect to spend 6–8 hours, ideally spread over three days (see the [study plan](/study-plan)).
